# TASK-0423: Blog articles overflow horizontally on mobile

## Problem
Blog article bodies scroll sideways on mobile (content runs off the right edge).
`body { overflow-x-hidden }` (app/layout.tsx) masks it on Android but leaks on iOS
Safari (documented bug), so the article still overflows on iPhone.

## Root cause
The markdown/prose container `.blog-content` and the `p`/`a` renderers had no
word-breaking. A single long unbroken token (a pasted URL, long domain, long word
in AZ/RU) forces the article wider than a 375px viewport.

## Fix (minimal, source-level)
- `app/globals.css` `.blog-content` — add `overflow-wrap: anywhere; word-break: break-word;`
  (the single highest-leverage fix; wraps long URLs/words in all rendered prose).
- `components/blog/MarkdownRenderer.tsx` — add `break-words` to the `p` (paragraph)
  and `a` (link) renderers (links are the worst offenders — raw URLs as link text).

Applies to both blog and news article bodies (both render through MarkdownRenderer/.blog-content).

## Not touched
No logic, no other components; formatter reflow deliberately avoided to keep a 4-line diff.
