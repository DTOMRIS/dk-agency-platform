# TASK-0566 — News run cap counts delivered drafts, not AI attempts

Owner: Doğan («haberler geldi ama 3 tane», 2026-11-27).

## Problem
Each run reserves one of its 15 budget slots *before* the AI pass. Since TASK-0561 the editor pass marks
title-only signals as unpublishable much more often:

| Run (UTC) | Tried | Unpublishable | Drafted → owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24.11 15:26 | 15 | 2 | 12 |
| 25.11 15:10 | 15 | 3 | 11 |
| 26.11 14:37 | 15 | 11 | 3 |
| 27.11 14:31 | 15 | 10 | 4 |

Unpublishable signals have a median source summary of 89 chars (drafted: 473). Filtering short signals
was rejected: over 21 days, 17 of 34 short-signal drafts were still approved by the owner.

## Change
- `lib/news/synthesize.ts`: the budget slot is reserved only after the editor pass returns a publishable
  draft. Unpublishable/error results no longer spend `RUN_SYNTHESIS_CAP`. Loop stops at 15 drafts, at
  `MAX_AI_ATTEMPTS_PER_RUN`, or when the day/run budget is full. `SOURCE_DAILY_CAP` counts drafts.
- `lib/news/pipeline-sql.ts`: `MAX_AI_ATTEMPTS_PER_RUN = 40` (~6 s each → ≤ ~4 min, job timeout 15).
- Budget SQL, daily cap 30 and delivery are unchanged. Overlapping runs are already prevented by the
  workflow `concurrency` group, so the slot no longer needs to act as a lock before the AI call.
