## 1. What is a Cookie?

A cookie is a small text file sent by a website to your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) and stored in your browser. Cookies help recognize you when you revisit the site, remember your preferences, and provide a better user experience.

## 5. How to Manage Cookie Consent?

### 5.1. Cookie Banner

When you first visit the site, the **Cookie Banner** appears. Through this banner, you can:

- **"Accept all"** — consent to all cookie categories;
- **"Only necessary"** — allow only mandatory cookies;
- **"Settings"** — make separate choices for each category.

### 5.2. Withdrawing Consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time:

- Via the **"Cookie Settings"** link in the footer;
- By deleting existing cookies in your browser settings.

### 5.3. Browser Settings

All modern browsers offer cookie management:

- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
- **Firefox:** Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
- **Edge:** Settings → Cookies and site permissions

### 5.4. "Do Not Track" Signal

If the "Do Not Track" feature is enabled in your browser, we **automatically disable analytics and marketing cookies**.
